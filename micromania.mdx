## Modes

| Mode | Available |
| --- | :-: |
| Normal | ✅ |
| Fast | — |
| Search | — |
| Generator | — |
| Wait | — |

Micromania runs on **Normal** only. There is no faster route and no bypass to fall back on, so your edge comes from the number of tasks, your proxies and the **Delay** you set.

There is no Generator on Micromania, so your accounts have to be created by hand and added in [Accounts](/accounts).

## Setting up a task

The task form is the same on every store — see [Tasks](/tasks) for what each field does. For Micromania:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the site">
    Select **Micromania** in the Site dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product">
    Paste the product URL or its SKU.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    **Normal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your groups">
    Profile group, account group and proxy group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Store-specific notes will be added here as they come up: proxy types that work best, delays that hold on a drop, and anything Micromania does differently.
</Note>
