Queue-IT is a virtual waiting room system used by many retail sites to manage traffic during high-demand drops. Stacked has built-in Queue-IT support, so your tasks automatically handle the queue flow and proceed to checkout without manual intervention.

## What Is Queue-IT?

Queue-IT places visitors in a virtual queue during high-traffic events such as sneaker drops, console restocks, and limited-edition launches. Users without a bot must wait their turn — often sitting in the queue for minutes or hours, only to find the item is sold out by the time they reach the product page. Stacked handles the entire Queue-IT flow automatically, keeping your task in the queue and moving to checkout the moment you advance.

## How Stacked Handles Queue-IT

Stacked manages every step of the Queue-IT flow on your behalf:

- Your task enters the Queue-IT virtual waiting room as soon as you start it
- Stacked tracks the queue token and monitors your position in real time
- When the queue advances and you reach the front, Stacked immediately navigates to the product page and initiates checkout
- The entire process is automated — you simply start the task before the drop and let Stacked do the rest

## Queue-IT Task Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Queue-IT store">
    Create a new task and choose a store that uses Queue-IT from the site selector. Check the [Supported Sites overview](sites/overview) if you're unsure which stores use Queue-IT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product URL or keyword">
    Paste the full product page URL into the task. If the product page isn't live yet before a pre-launch drop, use a keyword instead so Stacked can locate the listing once it goes live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Task Mode to Monitor + Checkout">
    In the task settings, set the Task Mode to **Monitor + Checkout** so Stacked continuously watches for queue entry and transitions to checkout automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a residential proxy group">
    Choose a residential proxy group from your proxy list. Residential proxies are strongly recommended for all Queue-IT sites — datacenter IPs are frequently identified and blocked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the task early">
    Launch your task 10–15 minutes before the drop begins. Entering the queue early gives you a better position and more time to reach the checkout before stock runs out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Queue-IT Sites

- Always use residential proxies — datacenter IPs are frequently blocked by Queue-IT before you even reach the product page
- Start tasks early to secure a better queue position ahead of the drop
- Run multiple tasks across different profiles and proxies to increase your chances of a successful checkout
- Keep your profiles updated with valid payment information so checkout completes as fast as possible once you exit the queue

<Warning>
  Queue-IT sites aggressively detect and block datacenter proxy IPs. Use residential or ISP proxies for the best results on any site protected by Queue-IT.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Stacked's Queue-IT compatibility is updated regularly as retailers adjust their configurations. Keep your bot up to date for the best performance on Queue-IT-protected drops.
</Note>
