Each retail site has its own checkout flow, anti-bot measures, and product page structure. This guide covers site-specific tips to help you get the most out of Stacked on each supported store.

<Accordion title="Amazon">
  **Best for:** electronics restocks, limited-edition collectibles, gaming hardware

  **Tips:**
  - Use the product ASIN in the URL for precise targeting — ASIN-based URLs are stable and won't break when Amazon updates its page structure
  - Enable auto-size selection for any items that require you to pick a size or variant before adding to cart
  - Use residential proxies during major launches to reduce the risk of session blocks on high-traffic product pages
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fnac">
  **Best for:** video games, Blu-rays, electronics, books, limited editions

  **Tips:**
  - Product URLs on Fnac are stable — paste the full product page URL directly into your task for reliable targeting
  - Queue-IT may be activated during major launches such as console releases or highly anticipated game drops; switch to residential proxies when this is the case
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cdiscount">
  **Best for:** electronics, household goods, toys

  **Tips:**
  - Use the full product URL copied directly from the Cdiscount product page for the most reliable task performance
  - Running multiple tasks with different profiles increases your overall checkout rate on competitive drops
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Smyths Toys">
  **Best for:** consoles, LEGO, action figures, collectibles

  **Tips:**
  - Smyths may activate Queue-IT during high-demand console launches or hot toy releases — always use residential proxies on this site
  - Check for region-specific stock availability; Smyths operates separate storefronts for the UK, Ireland, and Germany, and stock levels can vary significantly between regions
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="JouéClub, La Grande Récré, King Jouet">
  **Best for:** toys, board games, seasonal releases

  **Tips:**
  - Create accounts on these sites ahead of time using Stacked's Account Generator for faster, smoother checkout when drops go live
  - Standard datacenter proxies work for most drops on these sites — no need to switch to residential unless you notice blocks
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Micromania">
  **Best for:** video games, consoles, gaming accessories

  **Tips:**
  - Monitor upcoming release dates and set up tasks in advance so you're ready the moment new listings go live
  - Use keyword monitoring when exact product URLs aren't yet published — Stacked will locate the listing automatically once it appears on-site
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Philibert">
  **Best for:** board games, RPGs, limited-edition tabletop releases

  **Tips:**
  - Philibert drops are often announced publicly in advance — set up your monitoring tasks early to maximise your chance of a successful checkout
  - Datacenter proxies generally work fine for Philibert; residential proxies are not required for this site
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Monoprix, Auchan, E.Leclerc, Cultura">
  **Best for:** general merchandise, books, seasonal collectibles

  **Tips:**
  - Use the full product URL for each task — category and search page URLs are not suitable for task targeting
  - These sites typically don't use Queue-IT, so a standard proxy setup is all you need
</Accordion>

## General Best Practices

- Always copy the direct product page URL rather than a category or search results page — task targeting is more reliable and faster on direct URLs
- Pre-generate accounts on your target stores before major drops so checkout can proceed immediately without account creation delays
- Test your proxies and profiles at least 24 hours before a drop to catch any issues before they matter
- Keep Stacked updated to ensure compatibility with the latest changes to each site's checkout flow

<Tip>
  If a task fails on a specific site, check the Stacked Discord for site-specific announcements. The team publishes updates whenever a supported store changes its checkout flow or anti-bot configuration.
</Tip>
