Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about Stacked. If you can't find what you're looking for, visit the Stacked Discord community for live support.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="What is Stacked?">
    Stacked is an automated purchasing bot that monitors and checks out limited-edition products — sneakers, streetwear, toys, electronics, and collectibles — on 14+ supported retail sites, faster than any manual attempt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get access to Stacked?">
    Stacked is sold through Whop. Visit the waitlist page at [whop.com/checkout/plan_tEmqMBDGY9VlX](https://whop.com/checkout/plan_tEmqMBDGY9VlX) to join and purchase a license when slots are available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Stacked available for Mac and Windows?">
    Yes, Stacked supports both Windows 10/11 and macOS 12+. Download the appropriate installer after purchasing your license.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need proxies to use Stacked?">
    Proxies are strongly recommended, especially for competitive drops and Queue-IT sites. Without proxies, running many tasks from one IP address may result in bans. See the [Proxies guide](/concepts/proxies) for setup details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I buy proxies?">
    Stacked does not supply proxies. Purchase residential or datacenter proxies from a third-party provider and paste them into Stacked's Proxy Manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What stores does Stacked support?">
    Stacked supports 14+ stores including Amazon, Fnac, Smyths Toys, Cdiscount, Auchan, E.Leclerc, Monoprix, JouéClub, La Grande Récré, King Jouet, Cultura, Micromania, Philibert, and Queue-IT-powered sites. See the [Supported Sites](/sites/overview) page for the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Queue-IT and does Stacked support it?">
    Queue-IT is a virtual waiting room system used by many retailers during high-demand drops. Stacked has built-in Queue-IT support — your tasks automatically manage the queue flow and proceed to checkout when your turn arrives.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run tasks on multiple stores at the same time?">
    Yes. Stacked supports running many tasks simultaneously across different stores — or multiple tasks on the same store with different profiles and proxies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Account Generator?">
    The Account Generator automatically creates accounts on supported retail sites. Pre-creating accounts means faster checkout during drops, as you can skip the registration step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My task keeps failing — what should I do?">
    Check the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) for step-by-step solutions. Common causes include expired proxies, incorrect profile data, outdated bot version, or site-specific checkout changes. Make sure Stacked is updated to the latest version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I update Stacked?">
    Stacked checks for updates automatically on launch. If an update is available, you'll see a notification banner — click it to install the latest version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my payment information safe?">
    Yes. Your profile and payment data are stored locally on your device. Stacked only transmits this information directly to the checkout page of the store you're targeting — it is never stored on Stacked's servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my license?">
    No. Licenses are non-transferable and tied to your account. Sharing or reselling your license key may result in permanent revocation.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
