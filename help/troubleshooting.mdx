If something isn't working as expected with Stacked, this guide covers the most common issues and how to fix them. For issues not covered here, reach out in the Stacked Discord.

<Note>
  Before contacting support, always check that you are running the latest version of Stacked — many issues are resolved in routine updates.
</Note>

## License & Activation Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="My license key is not accepted">
    - Make sure you are copying the full key with no extra spaces.
    - Ensure you are logged in to Stacked with the same email used to purchase on Whop.
    - If the issue persists, contact Stacked support via Discord with your purchase receipt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stacked won't launch or crashes on startup">
    - Check that your system meets the minimum requirements (Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+, 8 GB RAM).
    - Try running the app as administrator (Windows) or granting the necessary permissions (macOS).
    - Re-download the installer from your purchase email and reinstall.
    - Check the Stacked Discord for any known outage announcements.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Task & Checkout Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="My tasks keep failing at checkout">
    - Verify your profile has valid and complete shipping and payment information.
    - Confirm your proxies are working by using the built-in proxy test.
    - Check that you are running the latest version of Stacked.
    - Try reducing the number of simultaneous tasks to avoid triggering rate limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tasks are stuck on 'Waiting for Product'">
    - Confirm the product URL is correct and the product page is accessible in a normal browser.
    - Check that the target site is not down or undergoing maintenance.
    - If using keyword mode, try switching to a direct product URL once it's available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tasks succeed but no order email received">
    - Check your spam/junk folder.
    - Verify the email address in your profile is correct.
    - Log in to the store's website to confirm whether an order was actually placed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Queue-IT tasks are failing or getting blocked">
    - Switch to residential or ISP proxies — datacenter IPs are frequently detected and blocked by Queue-IT.
    - Ensure you are starting your tasks at least 10–15 minutes before the drop.
    - Update Stacked to the latest version in case of Queue-IT compatibility changes.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Proxy Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Proxies show as failed or slow">
    - Test proxies using the built-in proxy tester before a drop.
    - Remove dead proxies from your list.
    - Contact your proxy provider if a large number of proxies are failing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Getting IP-banned on a site">
    - Use residential proxies instead of datacenter proxies for competitive stores.
    - Reduce the number of simultaneous tasks per proxy.
    - Rotate proxies between sessions by refreshing your proxy list.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Notification Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Not receiving SMS or email notifications">
    - Check that notifications are enabled in **Settings → Notifications**.
    - Verify your email address and phone number are correct.
    - For SMS, confirm your phone number is verified — re-send the verification code if needed.
    - For email, check your spam/junk folder.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Still Need Help?

If your issue isn't covered here, the Stacked community Discord is the fastest way to get support. The team and community members are active and can help diagnose site-specific or account-specific issues. You can also review announcements for known issues and upcoming fixes.
