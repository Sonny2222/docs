Setting up Stacked takes three steps: download the app, install the Chrome extension, and activate your license key.

## 1. Download Stacked

The Stacked desktop app and the Chrome extension are both distributed through the Stacked Discord.

<Card title="Open the download channel" icon="discord" href="https://discord.com/channels/834213844158513193/1301728853768667227">
  Find the latest build of the desktop app and the Chrome extension.
</Card>

<Note>
  Access to this channel is granted with your license. If you cannot open the link, make sure you have joined the Stacked Discord with the account linked to your purchase.
</Note>

## 2. Install the Chrome extension

The Chrome extension works alongside the desktop app and is what allows you to check out through **checkout links**. Install it before your first drop, because checkout links will not work without it.

## 3. Activate your license key

Your activation key lives in your Whop dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Whop dashboard">
    Go to [your Stacked dashboard on Whop](https://whop.com/stacked-io/exp_ezvSB3kr3vjjLd/app/) and sign in with the account you used to purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your activation key">
    Copy the key exactly as shown, with no extra spaces before or after it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Stacked">
    Launch Stacked and paste the key when prompted. Once validated, the app unlocks and you are ready to set up your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never share your activation key. It is tied to your Whop account, and sharing it can get your license revoked.
</Warning>

## macOS: if Stacked is blocked on first launch

macOS may block Stacked the first time you open it. This is standard behaviour for apps downloaded outside the App Store and does not mean anything is wrong with the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open System Settings">
    Click the Apple menu and choose **System Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Privacy & Security">
    Select **Privacy & Security** in the sidebar, then scroll down to the **Security** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Stacked to run">
    You will see a message reading *"StackedIO was blocked..."*. Click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Stacked will now launch normally every time.
