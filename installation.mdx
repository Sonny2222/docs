Stacked is a desktop application that runs directly on your computer, giving you full local control over your tasks and profiles. Before you can start copping drops, you need to install the app and activate it with the license key you received after purchase. The entire setup process takes just a few minutes.

## System Requirements

Make sure your machine meets the following requirements before installing Stacked:

| Requirement | Minimum / Recommended |
|---|---|
| **Operating System** | Windows 10 / Windows 11, or macOS 12 (Monterey) and above |
| **RAM** | 8 GB recommended (4 GB minimum) |
| **Disk Space** | 500 MB of free storage |
| **Internet Connection** | Stable broadband connection required |
| **Additional** | Administrator privileges for installation |

<Info>
  Running Stacked on a dedicated machine or a low-latency server environment can improve drop performance. A stable, fast internet connection is especially important during high-traffic releases.
</Info>

## Download & Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your purchase confirmation email">
    Find the confirmation email sent by Whop after your purchase. It contains your personal **download link** — click it to begin downloading the Stacked installer for your operating system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the installer">
    - **Windows:** Double-click the downloaded `.exe` file to launch the setup wizard.
    - **macOS:** Open the downloaded `.dmg` file, then drag the Stacked icon into your **Applications** folder.

    If your system displays a security warning, follow your OS prompts to allow the installer to run (on macOS, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the installation wizard prompts">
    Work through the on-screen steps to choose your installation directory and confirm the setup. The process takes under a minute on most machines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch Stacked">
    Once installation is complete, open Stacked from your **Desktop** shortcut (Windows) or your **Applications** folder (macOS). The app will launch and prompt you to activate your license on first run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate Your License

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Activate License' on the welcome screen">
    When you open Stacked for the first time, you'll be greeted by the activation screen. Click the **Activate License** button to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your license key">
    Copy your license key from your Whop purchase confirmation email and paste it into the activation field. Make sure there are no extra spaces before or after the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm to verify and unlock">
    Click **Confirm**. Stacked will connect to the licensing server, validate your key, and unlock the full application. This takes just a few seconds. Once verified, you'll land on the main dashboard and you're ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update the App

Stacked regularly ships updates to add new store integrations, improve checkout speeds, and patch issues. When a new version is available, you'll see an **update notification banner** at the top of the dashboard — click it to download and apply the update automatically. You can also check for updates manually from the **Settings** menu inside the app. Always keep Stacked up to date to ensure compatibility with the latest retailer changes and anti-bot measures.

<Warning>
  Never share your license key with anyone. Your key is tied directly to your Whop account and the device it was activated on. Sharing it may result in your license being permanently revoked with no possibility of refund.
</Warning>

Now that Stacked is installed and activated, you're ready to set up your first tasks. Head to the [Tasks](/concepts/tasks) guide to learn how to configure and run your first automated checkout.
