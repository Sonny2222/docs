<Note>
  Maison de la Presse checks out as a **guest**. No account is needed, so there is nothing to set up in [Accounts](/accounts) and no account group to attach to your tasks.
</Note>

## The queue

Maison de la Presse puts a **waiting queue** in front of its drops, so a task does not go straight to checkout. It takes its place in line first, and buys once it is through.

That is what separates the two modes:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Normal** | Waits in the queue, then checks out as soon as it comes out the other side. |
| **Fast** | The quicker route. You will see what it does on the drop. |

## Modes

| Mode | Available |

There is no Generator here, and there is no need for one: checkout is done as a guest.

## Checkout

Maison de la Presse supports **credit card auto checkout**: Stacked completes the payment itself, with no action from you once a task is through the queue.

## Setting up a task

The task form is the same on every store — see [Tasks](/tasks) for what each field does. For Maison de la Presse:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the site">
    Select **Maison de la Presse** in the Site dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product">
    Paste the product URL or its SKU.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    **Normal** to queue and check out, **Fast** for the quicker route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your groups">
    Profile group and proxy group. Leave the account group on **None**, since checkout is done as a guest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Because every task has to queue, start them early. A task created after the queue has opened starts from the back of the line.
</Tip>
