The Stacked account generator automates the process of creating accounts on supported retail sites, giving you a bank of ready-to-use accounts to speed up checkout. Instead of registering mid-drop — when every second counts — you arrive with accounts already in place and ready to go.

## Why Generate Accounts?

Pre-created accounts give you a meaningful edge during high-traffic drops:

- **Skip registration at checkout** — signed-in users bypass the account creation step entirely, shaving seconds off your checkout time.
- **Reduce friction** — saved addresses and payment preferences auto-fill at checkout, making the flow faster and less error-prone.
- **Access faster checkout paths** — some supported stores offer accelerated or one-click checkout exclusively for logged-in users.
- **Spread risk across accounts** — using multiple accounts across your tasks means a single flagged account doesn't kill your entire run.

## Supported Sites for Account Generation

Account generation is available for a select subset of Stacked's supported stores. Visit the [Sites Overview](/sites/overview) page for the full list of supported stores and their account generation status.

## Generating Accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Account Generator tab">
    In the Stacked dashboard, click the **Account Generator** tab in the left-hand navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the target site">
    Use the **Site** dropdown to choose the retail store you want to generate accounts for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the number of accounts">
    Enter how many accounts you want to generate in the **Quantity** field. Start with a smaller batch (10–20) to verify everything is working before scaling up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a proxy group">
    Choose a **Proxy Group** to route generation traffic through. Using proxies is strongly recommended to prevent your IP from being flagged or blocked by the target site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    Click the **Generate** button to begin. Stacked starts creating accounts in the background using the details and proxies you've configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor progress">
    Watch the **Status Panel** at the bottom of the Account Generator tab. It shows accounts created, any failures, and the overall progress of the generation batch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review generated accounts">
    Once the batch completes, your new accounts appear in the **Accounts** list. Each entry shows the site, username, creation date, and current status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Generated Accounts

Once accounts are generated, you can assign them directly to your tasks for a logged-in checkout flow:

1. Open a task in the **Tasks** tab.
2. In the task settings, find the **Account** field.
3. Select the account you want to use from the dropdown (filtered by site).
4. Save the task.

When the task runs, Stacked logs in with the assigned account before proceeding to cart and checkout. This gives you the speed and checkout-path benefits of a signed-in session without any manual login step.

## Account Health

Not all generated accounts stay healthy indefinitely. Keep an eye on the following:

- **Email verification** — some sites send a verification email before the account is fully active. Check your catch-all or generated inbox and verify accounts before drop day.
- **Flagged accounts** — accounts created too quickly or without proxies may be flagged or soft-banned by the site. Flagged accounts show a warning status in your Accounts list.
- **Refresh before drops** — run a quick health check on your accounts in the hours before a major drop to confirm they are still active and able to log in.

<Warning>
  Generating large volumes of accounts too quickly without proxies may trigger site-level bans on your IP address. Always use a residential proxy group for account generation to keep your activity clean.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Generate accounts a day or two before a major drop so they are aged and less likely to be flagged by the site's anti-fraud systems.
</Tip>
