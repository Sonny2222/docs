Stacked's notification system keeps you informed in real time — receive SMS or email alerts the moment a task successfully checks out, so you never miss a cop even when you're not watching the dashboard. You can configure global notification settings or fine-tune alerts on a per-task basis.

## Notification Types

| Notification | When It Fires |
|---|---|
| **Successful Checkout** | A task completes checkout and an order is placed successfully |
| **Task Failure** | A task encounters an error it cannot recover from automatically |
| **Product In Stock** | A monitored product first becomes available on the target site |
| **Drop Starting Soon** | An optional reminder sent before a scheduled drop time |

## Setting Up Email Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon in the Stacked dashboard sidebar to open the settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notifications">
    Select the **Notifications** tab within Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Email Notifications on">
    Click the **Email Notifications** toggle to enable it. The toggle turns green when active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address where you want to receive alerts into the **Email Address** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** to store your email address and activate email notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test notification">
    Click **Send Test Email** to trigger a test message. Check your inbox to confirm delivery. If it doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam folder and verify the address is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting Up SMS Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle SMS Notifications on">
    In the **Notifications** settings panel, click the **SMS Notifications** toggle to enable it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your phone number">
    Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field, including your country code — for example, `+33612345678` for a French number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send Verification Code">
    Click **Send Verification Code**. Stacked sends a one-time code to the number you entered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the verification code">
    Type the code from the SMS into the **Verification Code** field in Stacked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify & Save">
    Click **Verify & Save** to confirm your number and activate SMS notifications. Your number is now verified and ready to receive alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Per-Task Notification Settings

You can override your global notification settings for any individual task. This lets you silence alerts for low-priority tasks while keeping full notifications active for high-value drops.

To configure notifications for a specific task:

1. Open the task from the **Tasks** tab.
2. Scroll to the **Notifications** section within the task settings.
3. Toggle individual event types (Checkout Success, Failure, In Stock) on or off as needed.
4. Click **Save** to apply your per-task preferences.

Per-task settings always take priority over your global notification configuration.

## Notification Webhooks

Stacked also supports Discord webhooks, letting you send checkout notifications directly to a channel in your Discord server. Webhook messages include rich checkout cards with order details such as the product name, size, store, and order confirmation number.

To set up a webhook:

1. Copy your Discord channel's **Webhook URL** from Discord's channel settings (Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks).
2. In Stacked, go to **Settings → Notifications → Webhook URL**.
3. Paste the webhook URL into the field.
4. Click **Save**.

Stacked will now post a checkout card to that Discord channel whenever a task triggers a notification event.

<Tip>
  Enable Discord webhooks to get rich checkout cards with order details sent directly to your server — perfect for sharing cops with your team or keeping a log of successful orders.
</Tip>
