Creating a task is the core workflow in Stacked. Every automated checkout attempt starts here — you define the target product, assign your checkout profile and proxy group, configure your preferences, and let Stacked handle the rest. Once a task is running, Stacked monitors the product and fires the checkout sequence the moment stock is available.

## Before You Start

<Note>
  Make sure you have the following in place before creating your first task:

  - ✅ A **proxy group** configured in the Proxies tab
  - ✅ At least one **profile** created with your shipping and payment details
  - ✅ The **target product URL or keyword** for the item you want to cop
</Note>

## Creating a Task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tasks tab">
    Launch Stacked and navigate to the **Tasks** tab in the main dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New Task">
    Click the **+ New Task** button in the top-right corner to open the task creation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your target store">
    Use the **Store** dropdown to select the retail site you want to target. Stacked supports 14+ stores including Fnac, Smyths Toys, Amazon, Cdiscount, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product URL or keyword">
    Paste the direct **product page URL** into the Product field. If the URL is not yet available (for an upcoming drop), enter a **keyword** that matches the product name — Stacked will search the site and target the matching listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your size or variant">
    Select the specific size or variant you want to purchase from the dropdown. Choose **Auto** to let Stacked automatically target your preferred size based on your profile settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quantity">
    Set **Quantity** to `1` for a single item. Increase this number if you want to attempt multiple units in the same checkout flow (subject to site limits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Profile">
    Choose the **Profile** to use at checkout. This profile supplies your name, shipping address, and payment details to the checkout form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Proxy Group">
    Assign a **Proxy Group** to the task. Using a proxy protects your IP from rate-limiting and improves success rates on high-traffic drops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure advanced options">
    Expand **Advanced Options** if needed. You can enable **Retry on Failure** to automatically restart the task after an error, and set the **Task Mode** (e.g. Monitor + Checkout or Checkout Only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    Click **Save** to add the task to your task list. The task appears in the dashboard with a **Ready** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Starting a Task

Once your task is saved, click the **Start** button next to it to begin execution. The status indicator updates in real time — you'll see it move through states such as **Monitoring**, **Carting**, **Checking Out**, and finally **Success** or **Failed**.

The Stacked dashboard refreshes automatically so you always have a live view of every running task. If a task hits an anti-bot challenge, Stacked handles it automatically without any input from you.

## Bulk Task Creation

To increase your chances on high-demand drops, you can run multiple tasks simultaneously against the same product. There are two ways to do this:

- **Duplicate a task** — right-click any existing task and select **Duplicate**. Assign a different profile and proxy group to the copy.
- **Bulk Create** — click the **Bulk Create** button, enter the number of copies you want, and Stacked generates multiple tasks at once. You can then assign profiles and proxies in a single batch edit.

Running multiple tasks with different profiles and proxies means each checkout attempt looks like a separate user, which significantly improves your overall success rate.

## Stopping Tasks

To pause a running task, click the **Pause** button next to it. The task holds its current state and resumes from where it left off when you click **Resume**.

To stop a task entirely, click the **Stop** button. This terminates the task immediately and resets its status to **Ready**. You can restart it at any time.

To stop all tasks at once, click **Stop All** in the toolbar at the top of the Tasks tab.

<Tip>
  For high-demand drops, create 5–10 tasks targeting the same product with different profiles and proxies to increase your checkout success rate.
</Tip>
