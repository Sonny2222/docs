Three things need to be in place before your first task: a Discord webhook for notifications, your proxies, and at least one captcha provider key. The webhook and the API keys both live in the bot's **Settings** tab.

## Webhook

Stacked posts checkout notifications to a Discord webhook, so you know the moment an order goes through.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the webhook in Discord">
    In your Discord server, open **Server Settings > Integrations > Webhooks**, then click **New Webhook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a channel and copy the URL">
    Choose the channel the notifications should land in, then click **Copy Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Stacked">
    Open **Settings** in Stacked, paste the URL into the webhook field, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a private channel. The webhook posts your successful checkouts, and that is not information you want public.
</Tip>

## Proxies

Stacked has a dedicated **Proxies** tab where you can create several proxy groups and pick which group a task uses.

Residential, ISP and datacenter proxies all work. There is no universal setup: every store reacts differently, and finding the right proxy type for each site is part of dialling in your own setup.

<Tip>
  Keep one group per provider or proxy type. Switching a task from one group to another then takes a second when a site starts behaving differently.
</Tip>

## API keys

Open **Settings** in Stacked. You will find two provider sections: **Captcha Providers** and **Antibot Providers**.

For each one, paste your key into the provider's field, click **Set Default** on the provider you want Stacked to use, then click **Save**. You only need keys for the providers you actually use.

### Captcha providers

- 2Captcha
- AntiCaptcha
- AYCD
- CapMonster
- CapSolver
- CaptchaAI

### Antibot providers

- **Hyper** — set as the default provider
- Disasm
