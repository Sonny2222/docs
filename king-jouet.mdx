## Modes

| Mode | Available |
| --- | :-: |
| Normal | ✅ |
| Fast | ✅ |
| Search | — |
| Generator | — |
| Wait | — |

**Fast** is the mode to run on King Jouet. Keep a few **Normal** tasks on the same drop as a fallback.

There is no Generator on King Jouet, so your accounts have to be created by hand and added in [Accounts](/accounts).

<Note>
  King Jouet has to be whitelisted in your Hyper and Disasm dashboards before you run a task on it. See [Requirements](/requirements).
</Note>

## Checkout

King Jouet gives you two routes, chosen in the **Payment method** field of the task:

| Payment method | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit card** | Auto checkout. Stacked completes the payment itself, with no action from you. |
| **CART** | Stacked sends you a link that drops you straight on the final payment step. You enter your card on the site and press pay. |

**CART** is the slower of the two, since it needs you at the keyboard. Keep it for the times it helps rather than as your default.

## Setting up a task

The task form is the same on every store — see [Tasks](/tasks) for what each field does. For King Jouet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the site">
    Select **King Jouet** in the Site dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product">
    Paste the product URL or its SKU.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    **Fast** for the drop itself, **Normal** on a few tasks as backup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your payment method">
    **Credit card** for a hands-off checkout, **CART** to finish the payment yourself. If you go with credit card, make sure the profile carries one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Store-specific notes will be added here as they come up: proxy types that work best, delays that hold on a drop, and anything King Jouet does differently.
