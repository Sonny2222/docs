Stacked licenses are sold exclusively through [Whop](https://whop.com/checkout/plan_tEmqMBDGY9VlX), the trusted platform for software subscriptions and digital tools. Because demand for Stacked is high and license slots are limited, access is managed through a waitlist — new batches open periodically, so acting quickly when slots become available is essential.

## Join the Waitlist

Follow these steps to secure your Stacked license:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Stacked Whop page">
    Go to [https://whop.com/checkout/plan_tEmqMBDGY9VlX](https://whop.com/checkout/plan_tEmqMBDGY9VlX) to view available plans and join the waitlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or log in to your Whop account">
    If you don't already have a Whop account, sign up with your email address. Existing users can log in directly. Your Whop account is where your license will be managed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your subscription plan and complete checkout">
    Review the available plan options on the Whop page, select the one that suits you, and complete the secure checkout process using your preferred payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email for your license key and download link">
    After a successful purchase, Whop will send a confirmation email containing your **license key** and a **download link** for the Stacked desktop application. Keep this email safe — you'll need both details to activate the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Purchase

Once your payment is confirmed, here's what to expect:

- **Download link** — Your confirmation email includes a direct link to download the Stacked installer for Windows or macOS.
- **License key** — A unique activation key is issued to your Whop account and included in the same email. This key ties the software to your account.
- **Community access** — Many license holders also receive an invitation to the Stacked Discord server or support community, where you can get help, share setups, and stay informed about new store releases and drop alerts.

If you don't receive your email within a few minutes of purchase, check your spam folder or contact Stacked support through Whop.

<Tip>
  Follow Stacked on social media to get notified the moment new license slots open. Batches sell out fast — being the first to know gives you the best chance of securing access.
</Tip>
