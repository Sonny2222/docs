## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Access">
    Join the waitlist and purchase a Tcktio license through Whop. Once approved, you'll receive your download link and activation key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install & Activate">
    Download the Tcktio desktop app, run the installer, and activate with your license key. No command-line setup required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Profiles & Proxies">
    Add your shipping and payment profiles, then connect proxies to keep your tasks running smoothly during high-traffic drops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Tasks & Cop">
    Target your desired product on any supported site, set your task parameters, and start — Tcktio handles the rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>
