Stacked is a desktop purchasing bot for Windows and macOS, built for Pokémon, One Piece and collectible drops. This guide takes you from installation to your first task.

## Get started in 4 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Stacked">
    Download the app, add the Chrome extension and activate your key. See [Installation](/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your requirements">
    Discord webhook, proxies, captcha and antibot providers. See [Requirements](/requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your profiles and accounts">
    The details Stacked checks out with, and the store accounts it signs in with. See [Profile](/profile), [Accounts](/accounts) and [Generator](/generator).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first task">
    Pick a site, a product and a mode, then start. See [Tasks](/tasks).
  </Step>
</Steps>
