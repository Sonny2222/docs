## Modes

| Mode | Available |
| --- | :-: |
| Normal | ✅ |
| Fast | — |
| Search | ✅ |
| Generator | ✅ |
| Wait | — |

**Search** finds the product from a keyword instead of a URL, which is what you want when the product page does not exist yet at the time you set your tasks up.

**Generator** is available: create your Monoprix accounts from the [Generator](/generator) instead of signing up by hand.

There is no Fast mode on Monoprix, so spread your tasks and tune the **Delay** rather than relying on a bypass.

## Setting up a task

The task form is the same on every store — see [Tasks](/tasks) for what each field does. For Monoprix:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the site">
    Select **Monoprix** in the Site dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the product">
    Paste the product URL or its SKU, or type a keyword if you are running **Search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the mode">
    **Normal**, or **Search** when you only have a keyword.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your groups">
    Profile group, account group and proxy group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Store-specific notes will be added here as they come up: proxy types that work best, delays that hold on a drop, and anything Monoprix does differently.
</Note>
