Stacked is the automated purchasing bot built for sneaker enthusiasts, resellers, and collectors who refuse to lose out on limited-edition drops. Whether you're after the latest sneaker release, a hyped streetwear collab, or a sold-out toy collectible, Stacked works in the background at machine speed — so you can focus on what matters.

## What Is Stacked?

Stacked is a desktop application that automates the checkout process across 14+ major retail stores. Instead of manually refreshing product pages, racing through checkout forms, and watching items sell out before you can click "Add to Cart," Stacked handles the entire flow for you — from product monitoring to order confirmation — in milliseconds.

Manual checkout simply can't compete with bot-speed execution. Stacked detects restocks and new drops the moment they go live, fills in your shipping and payment details automatically, and submits your order before the competition even reacts.

Stacked covers a wide range of product categories, including:

- **Sneakers & footwear** — limited-edition releases and collabs
- **Streetwear & apparel** — hyped brand drops and capsule collections
- **Toys & collectibles** — LEGO, Pokémon, and other sought-after items
- **Electronics** — GPUs, consoles, and tech drops
- **Books, games & media** — exclusive editions and pre-order releases
