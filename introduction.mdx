Stacked is a desktop purchasing bot for Windows and macOS, built for limited-edition releases — Pokémon, One Piece, and other trading card and collectible drops.

## What is Stacked?

Stacked is an application you install on your own computer. It watches the stores you target and, the moment a release goes live, runs the checkout for you — faster than you could do it by hand.

Stacked runs on both **Windows** and **macOS**.

## Who it is for

Stacked is built for **resellers** who need to secure stock reliably on release day, and for **collectors** tired of losing every drop to faster buyers.
